Brew Like a Barista

Elevate your daily ritual with tools that bring out the best in every bean.

Drip Coffee Maker

$170

A classic countertop machine that drips hot water over ground coffee into a carafe.

Usage Tips:

  • Use medium grind coffee.
  • Clean the filter basket and carafe daily to avoid bitterness.
  • Preheat the carafe for better temperature retention.

French Press

$220

A manual brewer that steeps coarse coffee grounds in hot water before plunging.

Usage Tips:

  • Use coarse-ground coffee to avoid sediment.
  • Brew for 4 minutes before plunging.
  • Stir halfway through brewing for even extraction.

Espresso Machine

$470

Forces hot water through finely ground coffee under pressure to create a concentrated shot.

Usage Tips:

  • Use fresh, finely ground beans (espresso grind).
  • Tamp the grounds evenly.
  • Clean the group head and portafilter regularly.

Pour-Over

$120

Manual brewing where hot water is poured over coffee in a filter cone.

Usage Tips:

  • Use medium-fine grind.
  • Pour in slow, circular motions.
  • Bloom the coffee for 30 seconds before continuing to pour.

AeroPress

$160

A portable brewer that uses air pressure to push hot water through coffee.

Usage Tips:

  • Use fine to medium-fine grind.
  • Try both standard and inverted methods.
  • Brew for 1–2 minutes for a strong, smooth cup.

Cold Brew Maker

$93

Steeps coarse coffee in cold water for 12–24 hours for a smooth, low-acid brew..

Usage Tips:

  • Use a 1:4 coffee-to-water ratio for concentrate.
  • Steep in the fridge, not at room temp.
  • Dilute with water or milk before serving.
